# Compact SUV 2026: supplier notes

What the purchasing team agreed for the 2026 Compact SUV, as far as it matters to the catalog.

## Audio

The premium audio system is made by Harlow Acoustics. The contract covers model year 2026 only and
names twelve speakers and a subwoofer under the cargo floor.

## The hybrid's battery

The battery pack of the hybrid is supplied by Voltwerk, from its plant in Dresden. Voltwerk also
supplies the battery cooling system, which is why the two are always ordered together.

## Wireless charging

The wireless phone charging pad was redesigned before launch after phones slid off it in testing.
The redesigned pad has a raised rubber edge, and it fits the center console of every trim.
