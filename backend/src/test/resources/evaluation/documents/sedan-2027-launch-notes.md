# Sedan 2027: launch notes

These notes record why the 2027 Sedan is offered the way it is.

## Trims in Europe

Europe gets the Luxury trim and not the Touring. The two overlap in price there once tax is added,
and research with buyers showed that they took the Luxury when both were on offer.

## Massaging seats

The massaging front seats are supplied by Brandt Sitzsysteme. They need the ventilated front seats,
because the two share one air supply inside the seat.

## The rear comfort package

The rear comfort package puts the heated rear seats and the rear climate control together. Buyers
of the earlier Sedan ordered one of the two without the other in fewer than one case in fifty.
