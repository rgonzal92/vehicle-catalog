# Compact SUV 2027: carryover notes

The 2027 Compact SUV carries the 2026 content over. These notes record what changes with the model
year, and why.

## Asia

Asia is added as a region for 2027. The new plant in Rayong starts building the Compact SUV in
January, which makes it possible to sell the car in Asia without shipping it from North America.

The Sport trim is not offered in Asia. Dealers there asked for two trims only in the first year,
and the Base and the Touring were the two they chose.

## The hybrid's battery

From model year 2027 the battery pack of the hybrid is supplied by Kestrel Cells. The contract with
the supplier of 2026 ended with that model year, and Kestrel Cells builds the pack in Rayong, next
to the car.

## New for 2027

Pearl White joins the paints for 2027, since the paint shop in Rayong has a booth for pearl
finishes. Traffic Jam Assist is new as well: it was held back a year so that it could be tested
on the roads the car is now sold on.
