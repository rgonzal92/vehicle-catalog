# Compact SUV 2026: launch notes

These notes record why the 2026 Compact SUV is offered the way it is. They are written for the
people who maintain its catalog.

## The hybrid

The hybrid powertrain was not part of the launch content. It follows with the autumn update,
because the battery plant in Dresden reached full output only in September. Until then every
battery pack it built was promised to the Sedan.

The hybrid comes with regenerative braking on every trim that has it. The two were engineered
together and are never sold apart.

## The Off-Road trim

The Off-Road trim is sold in North America only. Certifying its raised suspension and its
all-terrain tires for Europe would have cost more than the trim was expected to earn there in the
model year. Europe is to be looked at again when the trim is next reworked.

## The panoramic roof

The panoramic roof is kept to the Touring trim. It needs the reinforced roof frame, and that frame
is built on one assembly line, which also builds every Touring.
