# Sedan 2027: product notes

Why the 2027 Sedan is offered the way it is. Written for the people who maintain its catalog.

## Trims by region

Europe gets the Base, the Sport, and the Luxury, and not the Touring. Once tax is added the
Touring and the Luxury cost nearly the same there, and buyers who were shown both took the Luxury.

Asia gets the Base, the Touring, and the Luxury, and not the Sport.

## Massaging seats

The massaging front seats need the ventilated front seats. The two share one air supply inside
the seat. They cannot be had with the sport seats, whose side bolsters leave no room for them.

## The rear comfort package

The rear comfort package puts the heated rear seats and the rear climate control together. Buyers
of the earlier Sedan ordered one without the other in fewer than one case in fifty.

## Traffic Jam Assist

Traffic Jam Assist needs lane centering, which keeps the car in its lane while it follows the one
ahead.
