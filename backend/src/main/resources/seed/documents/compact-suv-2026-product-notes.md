# Compact SUV 2026: product notes

Why the 2026 Compact SUV is offered the way it is. Written for the people who maintain its catalog.

## The hybrid

The hybrid powertrain was not part of the launch content. It came with the autumn update, which is
the catalog's second version, because the battery plant reached full output only in September.

The hybrid always comes with regenerative braking and with the battery cooling system. The three
were engineered together and are not sold apart.

The hybrid is not offered with the manual transmission. The gearbox has no room for the electric
motor.

## The Off-Road trim

The Off-Road trim is sold in North America only. Certifying its off-road suspension and its
all-terrain tires for Europe would have cost more than the trim was expected to earn there.

## The panoramic roof

The panoramic roof is kept to the Touring trim. It needs a reinforced roof frame, and that frame
is built on the line that builds the Touring.
