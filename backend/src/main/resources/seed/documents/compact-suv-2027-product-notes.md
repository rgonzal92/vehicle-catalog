# Compact SUV 2027: product notes

The 2027 Compact SUV carries the 2026 content over. These notes say what changed with the model
year, and why.

## Asia

Asia is a new region for 2027. A second plant starts building the Compact SUV in January, which
makes it possible to sell the car in Asia without shipping it across the Pacific.

Asia gets two trims, the Base and the Touring. Dealers there asked for a short list in the first
year, and those were the two they chose.

## What is new

Traffic Jam Assist is new for 2027. It was held back a year so that it could be tested on the
roads of every region the car is sold in. It needs adaptive cruise control, which it builds on.

Pearl White joins the paints, and a 12-inch infotainment display joins the 8-inch and the 10-inch.

## The hybrid

The hybrid can now be ordered on the Sport trim in North America as well. In 2026 the plant could
build it for the Touring only there.
